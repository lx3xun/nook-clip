# Paste Stack (`⌥ ⏎`)

When filling out forms or copying multiple fields (such as Name, Phone, Email, and Address) from a webpage into a spreadsheet or admin portal, switching windows back and forth for every single field is tedious and error-prone.

**With Paste Stack, you copy everything once, switch to your target window, and press native `⌘ V` repeatedly to deal out each item in order.**

---

## Three Ways to Start a Paste Stack

### 1. Multi-Select from History (Most Common)

If the items are already in your NookClip history:

1. Press `⌘ ⇧ V` to summon the panel.
2. Hold `Shift + ↑/↓` (or drag with your mouse) to select the items you want to paste.
3. Press **`⌥ ⏎`** (Option + Return).
4. A compact floating queue pill appears at the bottom of your screen — Paste Stack is ready!

> 💡 **FIFO Order**: NookClip automatically queues selected items in the chronological order you originally copied them (oldest first), matching the natural reading order of your source document.

### 2. Live Queue Recording (`⌃ ⌥ V`)

If you are about to gather scattered snippets from a long document or webpage:

1. Press the global shortcut **`⌃ ⌥ V`** (Control + Option + V) from any app.
2. An empty Paste Stack floating pill appears on screen.
3. Select text and press **`⌘ C`** normally in your source window — each copied snippet flies into the queue automatically.
4. Switch to your target app and press `⌘ V` to start pasting.

### 3. Smart Form Splitter (Tables & Contact Blocks)

When you copy a multi-column spreadsheet row, key-value block, or shipping contact line:

1. A lightweight **"Form Fill"** pill appears near your cursor showing the detected structure and field count.
2. Click the pill (or press **`⌥ ⏎`**) to split the block into individual fields and load them directly into the Paste Stack.
3. You can also select any structured entry in the main panel and press **`⌥ ⏎`** to split and queue it (for ordinary single text items, `⌥ ⏎` still pastes plain text).
4. You can toggle this prompt anytime under **Settings → Capture → Smart Form Fill**.

---

## How to Paste from the Queue

Once the queue is active, you don't need to open NookClip at all:

1. Focus the first input field in your target app (Excel, browser form, Notion, IDE).
2. Press native **`⌘ V`** — the first item is pasted!
3. Press `Tab` to move to the next field, then press **`⌘ V`** again — the second item is pasted!
4. Once the last item is pasted, the floating queue pill dismisses itself automatically.

### Floating Queue Controls

- **Progress Indicator**: Shows live progress such as `2 / 5 pasted`.
- **Next Up Marker**: The row with the green **`▶`** arrow is currently loaded in your clipboard.
- **Jump Out of Order**: Click any row to focus it as the next item, or click the **Paste** button on a specific row to paste it immediately.
- **Never Steals Focus**: The floating pill is a non-activating panel — your cursor stays inside your target input field at all times.
- **Draggable**: Drag the pill anywhere on screen; NookClip remembers its position.

---

## Dismissing the Queue Early

To stop before finishing the queue:

- Press **`Esc`** in any window, or
- Press **`⌃ ⌥ V`** again, or
- Click the close button `✕` on the floating pill.
