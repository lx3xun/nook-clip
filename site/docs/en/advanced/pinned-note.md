# Pin to Screen (`⌘ P`)

When writing code, reconciling spreadsheets, or referencing a screenshot or color palette, switching windows with `⌘ Tab` breaks your flow.

**Pin to Screen (`⌘ P`) turns any clipboard entry into a sleek floating reference card that stays on top of all windows.**

---

## 1. How to Pin an Entry (`⌘ P`)

1. Press `⌘ ⇧ V` to open NookClip.
2. Select any code snippet, text note, image, or color swatch.
3. Press **`⌘ P`** (Command + P).
4. The entry immediately floats on top of your workspace as a compact pinned note.

> 💡 **In-Place Replacement**: Pressing `⌘ P` on another entry replaces the existing pinned note in place, keeping your screen tidy.

---

## 2. Pinned Note Capabilities

### Rich Formatting Preserved
- **Code Notes**: Retains syntax highlighting and monospaced formatting.
- **Image Notes**: Displays screenshots or mockups in a clean frameless canvas with resizable bounds.
- **Color Notes**: Shows the live color swatch alongside HEX / RGB values.

### Opacity Wheel & Collapse
- Hold **`⌥` (Option) + Mouse Scroll Wheel** over the note (or drag the header slider) to smoothly adjust opacity between `20%` and `100%`, letting you read windows underneath.
- Click the collapse button on the header bar to fold the note into a single-line title bar when not actively looking at it.

### Click-Through Lock Mode
When typing or clicking in an editor directly beneath the pinned note:

1. Click the **Lock icon** in the top-right corner of the note to enable **Click-Through Mode**.
2. Mouse clicks and scrolls now pass straight through the floating note to the window underneath.
3. **Restore Interaction**: Press the global shortcut **`⌘ ⌥ P`** (Command + Option + P) or click the corner unlock badge to exit click-through mode.

### Inline Draft Editing
- Click inside any text note to edit its contents or jot down quick adjustments.
- Click **Save** at the bottom to store the modified text back into your clipboard history as a new entry.

---

## 3. Closing a Pinned Note

- Click the `✕` close button in the top corner of the note, or
- Focus the note and press **`Esc`**.
